# Revenue Operations Metric Dictionary

This document defines the core commercial metrics used in the Sales Pipeline Analytics project.

The purpose of the metric dictionary is to ensure that reporting uses consistent definitions and that business stakeholders can understand how each metric is calculated.

---

## Metric Definitions

| Metric                 | Definition                                              | Calculation / Logic                             | Primary Source         | Business Purpose                                           |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Total Opportunities    | Number of unique sales opportunities                    | `COUNT(DISTINCT opportunity_id)`                | Sales Pipeline         | Measures total opportunity volume                          |
| Won Opportunities      | Opportunities with a final stage of Won                 | `deal_stage = 'Won'`                            | Sales Pipeline         | Measures successfully closed deals                         |
| Lost Opportunities     | Opportunities with a final stage of Lost                | `deal_stage = 'Lost'`                           | Sales Pipeline         | Measures unsuccessful closed deals                         |
| Open Opportunities     | Opportunities still in the Engaging stage               | `deal_stage = 'Engaging'`                       | Sales Pipeline         | Measures active pipeline volume                            |
| Won Revenue            | Total closing value of Won opportunities                | `SUM(close_value)` where `deal_stage = 'Won'`   | Sales Pipeline         | Measures revenue associated with successfully closed deals |
| Closed Win Rate        | Percentage of completed opportunities that were won     | `Won / (Won + Lost) × 100`                      | Sales Pipeline         | Measures sales conversion effectiveness                    |
| Average Won Deal Value | Average closing value of Won opportunities              | `Won Revenue / Won Opportunities`               | Sales Pipeline         | Measures average value of successful deals                 |
| Pipeline Age           | Number of days an open opportunity has remained active  | `DATEDIFF(DAY, engage_date, as_of_date)`        | Sales Pipeline         | Measures age of active opportunities                       |
| 0–30 Day Pipeline      | Open opportunities aged up to 30 days                   | `age_days <= 30`                                | Sales Pipeline         | Identifies relatively recent pipeline                      |
| 31–60 Day Pipeline     | Open opportunities aged between 31 and 60 days          | `age_days BETWEEN 31 AND 60`                    | Sales Pipeline         | Monitors maturing opportunities                            |
| 61–90 Day Pipeline     | Open opportunities aged between 61 and 90 days          | `age_days BETWEEN 61 AND 90`                    | Sales Pipeline         | Identifies opportunities approaching follow-up threshold   |
| 90+ Day Opportunities  | Open opportunities active for more than 90 days         | `age_days > 90`                                 | Sales Pipeline         | Creates a follow-up population for older pipeline          |
| Regional Won Revenue   | Won revenue grouped by regional office                  | `SUM(close_value)` by region where stage = Won  | Pipeline + Sales Teams | Compares commercial contribution across regions            |
| Product Won Revenue    | Won revenue grouped by product                          | `SUM(close_value)` by product where stage = Won | Pipeline + Products    | Measures revenue contribution by product                   |
| Account Won Revenue    | Won revenue grouped by customer account                 | `SUM(close_value)` by account where stage = Won | Pipeline + Accounts    | Measures customer revenue contribution                     |
| Account Revenue Share  | Percentage of total won revenue generated by an account | `Account Won Revenue / Total Won Revenue × 100` | Reporting View         | Identifies revenue concentration                           |

## Reporting Grain

The primary reporting grain is:

**One row per opportunity**

This grain must be preserved when opportunity data is joined with product, account, and sales-team lookup tables.

Failure to preserve this grain could duplicate opportunities and inflate metrics such as opportunity counts or revenue.

---

## Pipeline Aging Logic

The dataset is historical rather than a live CRM extract.

For that reason, pipeline age is calculated using a dataset-based snapshot date instead of the current system date.

The snapshot date is derived from the latest available closing date in the reporting dataset.

Pipeline-aging groups are defined as:

| Age               | Classification     |
| ----------------- | ------------------ |
| 0–30 days         | Recent             |
| 31–60 days        | Developing         |
| 61–90 days        | Aging              |
| More than 90 days | Follow-up required |

An opportunity being more than 90 days old does **not automatically mean that it is invalid or lost**.

The classification is intended to identify opportunities that should be reviewed by Sales or Revenue Operations.

## Data Quality Rules

The following validation rules are applied before commercial metrics are used:

**Opportunity ID:** Expected to uniquely identify an opportunity.

**Product mapping:** Product values should match the approved product reference table.

**Sales-agent mapping:** Sales agents should map correctly to the Sales Teams dataset.

**Account mapping:** Account IDs should map to the Accounts dataset where an account is available.

**Closing value:** Negative closing values are investigated as potential data-quality issues.

**Dates:** Closing dates should not occur before valid engagement dates.

**NULL closing values:** Acceptable for open opportunities that have not yet reached a final outcome.

**NULL closing dates:** Acceptable for open opportunities without a final outcome.

## Metric Interpretation Notes

### Closed Win Rate

The project calculates:

**Won ÷ (Won + Lost)**

Open opportunities are excluded because they have not yet reached a final outcome.

This prevents unfinished opportunities from artificially reducing the closed-deal win rate.

### Revenue

Revenue in this project refers to the recorded closing value of Won opportunities.

It should not be interpreted as:

* Profit
* Gross margin
* Recognized accounting revenue
* Recurring revenue

unless the original business dataset explicitly defines it that way.

### Open Pipeline

The dataset does not contain a reliable expected monetary value for every currently open opportunity.

For this reason, open-pipeline analysis focuses primarily on:

* Opportunity volume
* Opportunity age
* Sales ownership
* Region
* Product
* Account

rather than presenting an unsupported pipeline-value estimate.

## Governance Principle

Before publishing or comparing a metric, Revenue Operations should confirm:

**Definition → Source → Reporting grain → Filters → Time period → Validation**

This helps ensure different dashboards and stakeholders are working from the same definition of the business.
